# Future Features (P2, not implemented)

These ideas are outside BiteSync V1 and must not be added during feature
freeze. Each requires a separate product decision and contract review.

- Rich cardio metrics such as distance, pace, speed, GPS, and heart rate.
- Training timers or countdown workflows.
- Uploaded/hosted training videos, thumbnails, processing, or transcoding.
- Multiple titled teaching videos per exercise and in-app playback.
- Image-upload infrastructure for profile avatars.
- Social feed, chat, notifications, and subscriptions.
- Additional AI analysis or coaching features.

The legacy mini-program's richer video objects and embedded WebView are not V1
requirements; V1 deliberately uses one external URL per exercise reference.
